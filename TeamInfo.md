## Project Summary

We aim to develop a comprehensive healthcare application that improves the experience for doctors. At its core, the application will feature a user-friendly appointment and billing management system for a clinic. It includes supplementary features such as digital prescription generation and health and clinic insights for doctors, as mentioned below.

To enhance efficiency and reduce paper waste, the app will allow access to digitized prescriptions and medical forms, providing patients with easy access to important documents. Additionally, doctors will be able to track the vitals history of patients, allowing them to monitor health trends over time. The system also offers financial insights, including income trends and billing history, to help them better understand and manage their practice performance effectively. Overall, the application addresses key pain points in healthcare delivery through digitization, transparency, and intuitive design.
